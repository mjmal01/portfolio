Privacy policy · last updated November 16, 2026

mae

mae is a personal tool that Molly Maloney built for her own use. It helps her sort and clean up her own Gmail inboxes. It is not offered to the public, and only accounts she owns sign in to it.

What it accesses

When you sign in with Google, mae asks for permission to read and organize Gmail (the gmail.modify scope). It reads message metadata (such as sender, subject, date and labels) so it can sort, label, archive and clean up messages in that inbox. It does not send email.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to sort and clean up the signed-in user's own inbox.
  • mae runs on Molly's own computer. Sign-in tokens and working data stay there.
  • No data is sold, shared, or sent to third parties.
  • When Molly asks one of her own AI assistants (such as Claude or ChatGPT, under her own account) to run a mae cleanup, the results she asks about pass through that assistant to show them to her. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
  • No data is used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

mae's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove mae's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
